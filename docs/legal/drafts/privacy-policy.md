## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

{{LEGAL_OPERATOR_NAME}}, trading as Rozaya Boutique ("we", "us", or "our"), is
responsible for the personal information we process to operate our storefront,
sell jewelry, fulfill orders, and respond to customers (the "Services"). Our
contact details are in section 12.

Our storefront is a custom website hosted on Vercel. Shopify provides our
commerce infrastructure, including product, cart, and checkout services. These
are different services; our entire website is not hosted by Shopify. For
eligible international transactions, Global-e and its service providers may
also process information for merchant-of-record, payment, customs, tax, and
delivery services, as identified in your checkout and order documents.

Some providers process information on our instructions, while others also act
independently for their own services or legal obligations. This policy describes
our practices; provider privacy notices explain their separate practices. It
does not replace the notices of Shopify, your payment provider, or Global-e.

## 2. Information we collect and its sources

Depending on how you interact with the Services, we receive information from
you, from your browser or device, and from providers supporting a transaction:

- **Contact and delivery information:** your name, email address, telephone
  number when supplied or required, billing and delivery addresses, and the
  details needed to communicate with you and deliver an order.
- **Shopping and transaction information:** products and variants selected,
  cart contents, orders, amounts, currency, payment status, refunds, returns,
  and fulfillment or delivery information.
- **Communications:** information you provide when asking a question, reporting
  an issue, or making a return or privacy request. Please do not send full card
  numbers, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.
- **Technical and usage information:** IP address, browser/device information,
  pages or requests made, approximate location derived from an IP address,
  security and diagnostic logs, and identifiers needed for cart and privacy
  choices. Optional tracking is subject to the controls described below.
- **Provider information:** payment confirmation and fraud indicators from
  commerce/payment services, delivery status from fulfillment services, and
  information needed to resolve an order or customer-service issue.

Payment information is entered into the payment or checkout provider's service.
Those providers may collect card and other payment credentials to process your
payment. That does not mean Rozaya Boutique receives or stores your complete
card number or security code. Our access to transaction information depends on
the payment method and provider. This policy does not claim that we collect
passwords, security-question answers, wishlists, reviews, or SMS subscriptions
through features that the custom storefront does not offer.

## 3. How and why information is used

We use information as needed to:

- Provide the storefront, remember a shopping cart, process payments and orders,
  calculate relevant charges, and arrange delivery, returns, and refunds.
- Respond to questions, provide order updates, and handle complaints or rights
  requests.
- Protect the Services and transactions against fraud, misuse, and security
  incidents; diagnose faults and maintain service reliability.
- Maintain accounting and transaction records, comply with legal obligations,
  and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
- Understand or improve the Services, and support relevant marketing or
  advertising where these activities are enabled and legally permitted,
  subject to applicable consent requirements and opt-outs.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on
performance of a contract or steps you request before contracting, compliance
with legal obligations, consent where required, and legitimate interests in
operating and protecting our business where those interests do not override
your rights. You may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was
lawful before withdrawal. Optional marketing consent is not a condition of
buying a product, and accepting Terms or browsing the site is not consent to
optional tracking.

## 4. Who receives information

We disclose information as needed for the purposes above to:

- Shopify, Vercel, and providers supporting hosting, commerce, payment
  processing, security, and technical operations.
- Global-e and relevant international transaction providers where applicable,
  and shipping carriers or fulfillment providers involved in your order.
- Providers of email and customer support used to communicate with you.
- Professional advisers, authorities, or other recipients where reasonably
  necessary to meet legal obligations, protect rights, or address fraud or
  security incidents, subject to applicable law.
- Parties to a proposed or completed business transaction, with appropriate
  safeguards and notices where required, or recipients you direct us to use.

We do not describe every disclosure as a "sale." Certain advertising-related
disclosures or cross-merchant uses can nevertheless be treated as a sale,
sharing, or targeted advertising under applicable privacy laws even when no
money changes hands. The choices in section 6 apply where relevant.

For these uses, Shopify may process information as an independent controller,

[Shopify's consumer privacy policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy/app-users)

themselves confirm completion of every account-level or server-side request.

## 6. Cookies, privacy choices, and GPC

Necessary technologies support the shopping cart, security, checkout, and
recording privacy choices. Optional preferences, analytics, and marketing
technologies are governed by applicable consent requirements and choices.
Declining optional cookies does not turn off necessary transaction or security
processing. For Shopify's technologies, see its
[cookie policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/cookies).

Use [Privacy choices]({{PRIVACY_CHOICES_URL}}) to reopen Shopify's cookie preferences
panel or submit a browser data sale/sharing opt-out. Links are also available
in the storefront footer. The automatic banner uses the regions configured in
Shopify rather than treating every visitor as being in the same jurisdiction.
Cookie-purpose consent and data sale/sharing opt-outs are separate choices.

Shopify's Customer Privacy API automatically processes Global Privacy Control
(GPC) signals in regions configured for data-sharing opt-outs. GPC is a browser
or device signal; it is not the same as an email unsubscribe or an authenticated
account-level request. Other browser "Do Not Track" settings are not necessarily
the same signal. Please contact us if you need assistance or want to make an
account-level request.

Browser choices can be affected by clearing browser data, using a different
browser/device, or visiting a different domain. You may need to set choices
again. Blocking scripts may prevent the controls from loading; if so, use the
contact details in section 12. We do not treat a loading failure as consent.

## 7. Communications

Order, delivery, security, and customer-service communications are distinct from
promotional messages. If you receive promotional email from us, you can use its
unsubscribe instructions or contact us. Opting out of promotional messages
does not stop necessary communications about an order or a request. This
policy does not subscribe you to email or SMS marketing.

## 8. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for
which it was collected, including completing orders and resolving issues,
maintaining legally required records, preventing fraud, and handling legal
claims. The relevant period depends on the type of information, the
transaction, applicable law, and a provider's separate obligations. A deletion
request may be subject to lawful retention exceptions.

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no method of
transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not send sensitive
payment credentials or passwords to customer support. Provider security and
retention practices are also described in their notices.

promise that every provider or processing operation is exclusively in the
United States. International orders can also involve destination-country
providers and authorities.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we or
the relevant provider must use an appropriate lawful mechanism. Contact us
for information about safeguards relevant to your request. This policy does
not represent that every provider uses a particular transfer mechanism without
verification of the applicable arrangements.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request
access to or a copy of information, correction, deletion, portability,
restriction of or objection to processing, and withdrawal of consent. You may
also have rights to opt out of sale/sharing or targeted advertising, limit
certain uses of sensitive information, use an authorized agent, or appeal a
decision on a request. These rights have legal conditions and exceptions.

Submit a request using the details in section 12. We may need proportionate
information to verify your identity or an agent's authority; please do not
send identity documents or payment credentials unless we explain why they are
needed and how to provide them securely. We will handle requests within the
time required by applicable law and will not unlawfully discriminate against
you for exercising a privacy right.

If an applicable law gives you a right to appeal, contact us identifying the
request and asking for an appeal. You may also complain to the relevant
privacy regulator, including your local European data-protection authority
or the UK Information Commissioner's Office where applicable. Shopify's
separate rights processes are linked in section 5.

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal
requirements. The effective date will identify the current version. We will
provide additional notice or obtain consent where legally required; a policy
